Privacy Policy

Gracelight CBT · Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: Gracelight CBT collects only what is needed to run tests, notes and assignments: a student’s name, the exam PIN or login details the centre gives them, a passport photograph uploaded by the student, their answers and their results. We do not sell personal data, we do not show advertisements, and the app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

1. Who we are

Gracelight CBT is the learning app of Gracelight Learning Centre, Oda (“we”, “us”, “our”). It is available as a website at https://gracelight-cbt.onrender.com and as an Android app (package name com.gracelight.cbt). Gracelight Learning Centre is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Student (candidate) name and the exam PIN or login details given by the centreTo let the right student sign in, take tests and see their own results
Passport photograph that the student chooses to upload before an examTo identify the candidate on the exam screen and on the result slip
Class or subject chosen, and test answers, scores, dates and timesTo mark tests, show results and keep learning records
The name a student types when submitting an assignment, and the work submittedTo let teachers see who submitted what
Teacher and administrator login detailsTo protect the admin area and let staff manage notes, questions, assignments and results
Basic technical information, such as device or browser type, IP address and time of access (kept in server logs)To keep the service secure and fix problems

We do not collect payment information, precise location, contacts, microphone or camera data, or SMS messages. The app does not ask for those permissions. A photograph is received only when a student chooses a file to upload as their passport photograph.

3. How we use information

  • To run computer-based tests, mark them and show results to the student and the student’s teachers.
  • To share notes and assignments and collect submissions.
  • To keep records of student progress at the centre.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent misuse and fix technical problems.

We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

4. Who can see the information

  • The student can see their own results.
  • Teachers and administrators of Gracelight Learning Centre can see students’ names, photographs, answers, scores and submissions.
  • The Check Result page shows a result slip (name, photograph, subject, topic, score and remark) when a candidate’s name is entered. Candidates are advised to enter a unique name (for example, with their registration number added) so that others cannot easily guess it, and not to share their result slip with people they do not trust.
  • Service providers that host the website and store the data for us (our cloud hosting provider, Render, and our cloud database provider). They process data only to provide the service.
  • Authorities, where we are required to do so by law.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Students under 18

Gracelight CBT is used by secondary-school and exam-preparation students who are taught at Gracelight Learning Centre. Student accounts and login details are created by the centre, and students use the app under the supervision of their teachers. The app is not intended for children under 13. A parent or guardian may contact us at any time to ask what we hold about their child, including their photograph, to correct it or to have it deleted.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The website and app may use browser storage (for example session storage or cookies) to keep a student or administrator signed in and to keep a test running smoothly. We do not use advertising cookies. The app’s service worker stores only the app icons and the manifest file, never test content or personal data.

7. Security

The website uses HTTPS, so information sent between your device and our server is encrypted. Access to the admin area requires a login, and only authorised staff of the centre can use it. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. How long we keep information

We keep student records, such as results and submissions, for as long as the student is enrolled and for a reasonable period afterwards for educational record-keeping. After that, we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your data earlier (see section 9).

9. Your rights and how to request deletion

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you or your child;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your data or your child’s data (including their results and submissions);
  • stop using your data in a particular way, or withdraw consent you gave us.

To make a request, email gracelightlearningcentre@gmail.com with the student’s full name and class. We will confirm your identity and reply within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

10. Links to other websites

Our notes may sometimes mention outside websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make important changes, we will tell students and parents through the centre.
